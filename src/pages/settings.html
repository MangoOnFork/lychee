Safety

Panic button, disguise, private and Tor tabs, and leaving no trace.

Panic key
Instantly jumps to a safe page, mutes every tab and hides the others from the tab strip. Press it again to come back.
Panic page
Window disguise
Changes the window title and taskbar icon, so Alt+Tab and the taskbar show something boring.
Private and Tor tabs
Private tabs keep no history, cookies or cache. Tor tabs also route everything through the Tor network, which gets past network blocks and hides your IP.
HTTPS-only mode
Upgrades every site to an encrypted connection, so your network can't read or tamper with pages. Asks before loading a site that only works over plain HTTP.
Pause history
Stop saving the sites you visit, in every tab.
Clear when Lychee closes
Wipe these every time you quit.
Confirm before closing
Ask before the window closes, so you don't lose tabs by accident.

Search engine

Used by the address bar and the home page search box.

Lychee Search
Gives the search pages Lychee's look, name, logo and a Google-style image sidebar. Turn off to see the original search site. (Credits in About.)

YouTube

Built-in upgrades for YouTube. They kick in automatically, no extensions needed.

Lychee theme on YouTube
YouTube follows your Lychee theme: background, colours, and an accent-coloured progress bar.
Ambient Light
The video's colours glow softly around the player, like a TV with backlights.
Skip and hide ads
Hides ad slots and promos, and mutes and skips video ads the moment they start. Works with the ad blocker in Privacy.

Network

Secure DNS
Looks sites up over encrypted DNS-over-HTTPS instead of asking your network's DNS server, like Brave's and Chrome's Secure DNS. Your network can't see or filter which sites you look up.
Strict mode
Never fall back to your network's normal DNS. If the secure server is blocked, sites won't load instead of leaking.
Browser identity
What Lychee tells websites it is (the user agent). Picking a phone makes most sites send their mobile version.
Proxy server
Leave empty to use your system settings. Examples: socks5://127.0.0.1:1080 http://proxy.example:8080 http=host:80;https=host:443
